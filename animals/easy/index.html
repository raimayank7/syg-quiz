Animals and Nature quiz: easy round

Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Which is the largest land animal?

    Show answer

    African elephant. The African bush elephant is the largest land animal.

  2. Which is the fastest land animal?

    Show answer

    Cheetah. A cheetah can run at about 100 km per hour for a short burst.

  3. Which animal is called the king of the jungle?

    Show answer

    Lion. The lion is called the king of the jungle.

  4. Which animal gives us wool?

    Show answer

    Sheep. Wool comes from the fleece of sheep.

  5. What is a baby dog called?

    Show answer

    Puppy. A baby dog is a puppy.

  6. How many legs does a spider have?

    Show answer

    8. Spiders have eight legs. Insects have six.

  7. Which is the tallest animal?

    Show answer

    Giraffe. The giraffe is the tallest animal.

  8. Which of these birds cannot fly?

    Show answer

    Penguin. Penguins cannot fly, but they swim very well.

  9. Which animal is called the ship of the desert?

    Show answer

    Camel. Camels carry people and goods across deserts.

  10. Which insect makes honey?

    Show answer

    Honeybee. Honeybees make honey from the nectar of flowers.
