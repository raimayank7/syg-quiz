Animals and Nature quiz: medium round

Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Which is the largest animal that has ever lived?

    Show answer

    Blue whale. The blue whale is larger than any known dinosaur.

  2. Which mammal can truly fly?

    Show answer

    Bat. Bats are the only mammals that can truly fly. Flying squirrels only glide.

  3. Which bird lays the largest egg?

    Show answer

    Ostrich. The ostrich lays the largest egg of any living bird.

  4. How many hearts does an octopus have?

    Show answer

    Three. An octopus has three hearts.

  5. What is a group of lions called?

    Show answer

    Pride. A group of lions is a pride.

  6. Which animal is famous for changing its colour?

    Show answer

    Chameleon. Chameleons can change the colour of their skin.

  7. Kangaroos are native to which country?

    Show answer

    Australia. Kangaroos live in Australia.

  8. What is a baby frog called?

    Show answer

    Tadpole. A young frog is a tadpole, and it lives in water.

  9. Which is the largest of the big cats?

    Show answer

    Tiger. The tiger is the largest cat.

  10. Which bird can fly backwards?

    Show answer

    Hummingbird. Hummingbirds can hover and fly backwards.
