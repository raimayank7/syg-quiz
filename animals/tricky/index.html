Animals and Nature quiz: tricky round

Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Which Indian state is the only wild home of the Asiatic lion?

    Show answer

    Gujarat. Asiatic lions live in and around the Gir forest in Gujarat.

  2. Kaziranga National Park, famous for the one-horned rhinoceros, is in which state?

    Show answer

    Assam. Kaziranga is in Assam.

  3. What is a group of crows called?

    Show answer

    A murder. A group of crows is called a murder. A parliament is a group of owls.

  4. Which animal has fingerprints very like a human's?

    Show answer

    Koala. Koala fingerprints look very similar to human ones.

  5. How many legs do insects have?

    Show answer

    Six. All insects have six legs.

  6. In a butterfly's life, which stage comes after the caterpillar?

    Show answer

    Pupa. The caterpillar becomes a pupa, also called a chrysalis.

  7. Which is the largest living reptile?

    Show answer

    Saltwater crocodile. The saltwater crocodile is the largest living reptile.

  8. Which of these mammals lays eggs?

    Show answer

    Platypus. The platypus and the echidnas are mammals that lay eggs.

  9. Which bird is the fastest when it dives?

    Show answer

    Peregrine falcon. A peregrine falcon can dive at more than 300 km per hour.

  10. What are animals that eat only plants called?

    Show answer

    Herbivores. Herbivores eat only plants.
