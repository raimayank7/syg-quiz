Human Body quiz: easy round

Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Which organ pumps blood around the body?

    Show answer

    Heart. The heart pumps blood through the whole body.

  2. Which organs do we use to breathe?

    Show answer

    Lungs. The lungs take in oxygen and remove carbon dioxide.

  3. How many senses are usually counted in the human body?

    Show answer

    Five. Sight, hearing, smell, taste and touch.

  4. Which organ controls the whole body?

    Show answer

    Brain. The brain controls thinking, movement and the other organs.

  5. How many teeth does an adult usually have?

    Show answer

    32. A full adult set has 32 teeth, counting the wisdom teeth.

  6. Where does food go straight after we swallow it?

    Show answer

    Stomach. Food goes down the food pipe into the stomach.

  7. What is the normal human body temperature?

    Show answer

    About 37 °C. Normal body temperature is about 37 °C, or 98.6 °F.

  8. Sunlight on the skin helps the body make which vitamin?

    Show answer

    Vitamin D. The skin makes vitamin D in sunlight.

  9. What do we call a doctor who looks after teeth?

    Show answer

    Dentist. A dentist looks after teeth and gums.

  10. How many milk teeth does a child have?

    Show answer

    20. Children have 20 milk teeth.
