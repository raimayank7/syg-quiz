Human Body quiz: medium round

Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. How many bones does an adult human have?

    Show answer

    206. An adult skeleton has 206 bones.

  2. Which is the largest organ of the human body?

    Show answer

    Skin. The skin is the largest organ.

  3. Which is the hardest substance in the human body?

    Show answer

    Tooth enamel. The enamel on teeth is harder than bone.

  4. Which blood cells carry oxygen?

    Show answer

    Red blood cells. Red blood cells carry oxygen using haemoglobin.

  5. Which is the longest bone in the body?

    Show answer

    Femur. The femur, the thigh bone, is the longest.

  6. How many chambers does the human heart have?

    Show answer

    Four. Two atria and two ventricles.

  7. In which part of the body is the smallest bone?

    Show answer

    Ear. The stapes in the middle ear is the smallest bone.

  8. People of which blood group are called universal donors?

    Show answer

    O negative. O negative red cells can be given to almost anyone in an emergency.

  9. Which organs filter the blood and make urine?

    Show answer

    Kidneys. The two kidneys filter waste from the blood.

  10. Which organ makes insulin?

    Show answer

    Pancreas. The pancreas makes insulin, which controls blood sugar.
