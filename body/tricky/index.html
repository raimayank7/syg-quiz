Human Body quiz: tricky round

Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. About how many bones does a newborn baby have?

    Show answer

    About 300. A baby has around 300 bones and bone parts. Many join together later.

  2. Which is the largest organ inside the body?

    Show answer

    Liver. The liver is the largest internal organ.

  3. How many pairs of ribs does a human have?

    Show answer

    12. There are 12 pairs, 24 ribs in all.

  4. What is the coloured part of the eye called?

    Show answer

    Iris. The iris gives the eye its colour. The pupil is the dark opening in its middle.

  5. How many chromosomes does a normal human body cell have?

    Show answer

    46. Human cells have 23 pairs, 46 in all.

  6. Which waste gas do the lungs remove from the blood?

    Show answer

    Carbon dioxide. We breathe out the carbon dioxide that the body makes.

  7. Which blood cells fight infection?

    Show answer

    White blood cells. White blood cells defend the body against germs.

  8. Which is the largest muscle in the body?

    Show answer

    Gluteus maximus. The gluteus maximus, in the hip, is the largest muscle.

  9. What is a normal resting heart rate for an adult?

    Show answer

    60 to 100 beats a minute. For most adults a resting heart beats 60 to 100 times a minute.

  10. What is the windpipe also called?

    Show answer

    Trachea. The trachea carries air to the lungs. The oesophagus is the food pipe.
