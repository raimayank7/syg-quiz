Festivals and Culture quiz: easy round

Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Which festival is known as the festival of lights?

    Show answer

    Diwali. Diwali is celebrated with lamps and lights.

  2. Which festival is known as the festival of colours?

    Show answer

    Holi. People play with colours on Holi.

  3. On which date is Christmas celebrated?

    Show answer

    25 December. Christmas is on 25 December.

  4. Which festival marks the end of the month of Ramzan?

    Show answer

    Eid-ul-Fitr. Eid-ul-Fitr is celebrated when the month of fasting ends.

  5. Flying kites is a big part of which festival in Gujarat?

    Show answer

    Makar Sankranti. Uttarayan, on Makar Sankranti, fills the sky with kites.

  6. Onam is the harvest festival of which state?

    Show answer

    Kerala. Onam is celebrated in Kerala.

  7. Pongal is the harvest festival of which state?

    Show answer

    Tamil Nadu. Pongal is celebrated in Tamil Nadu in January.

  8. Baisakhi is a harvest festival mainly of which state?

    Show answer

    Punjab. Baisakhi is celebrated in Punjab in April.

  9. Durga Puja is celebrated most grandly in which state?

    Show answer

    West Bengal. Durga Puja is the biggest festival of West Bengal.

  10. On which festival does a sister tie a rakhi on her brother's wrist?

    Show answer

    Raksha Bandhan. The thread is tied on Raksha Bandhan.
