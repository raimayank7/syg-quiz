Quiz

Festivals and Culture quiz questions with answers

Thirty questions on the festivals, dances and arts of India, from Bihu to Onam and from Kathak to Warli. A good round for festival get-togethers and school cultural weeks.

  • Easy round

    Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more.

    • Which festival is known as the festival of lights?
    • Which festival is known as the festival of colours?
    • On which date is Christmas celebrated?
    Play this round

  • Medium round

    Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams.

    • Bihu is the main festival of which state?
    • Ganesh Chaturthi is celebrated on the largest scale in which state?
    • Kathakali is a classical dance of which state?
    Play this round

  • Tricky round

    Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish.

    • Kathak is a classical dance that grew in which part of India?
    • Odissi is a classical dance of which state?
    • The famous camel fair of Pushkar is held in which state?
    Play this round

How to play

Open a round and tap the option you think is right. The answer and a short note appear at once, and your score is counted at the bottom. To play with a group, send the round on WhatsApp and paste the answer key when the time is up.
