Festivals and Culture quiz: medium round

Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Bihu is the main festival of which state?

    Show answer

    Assam. Bihu is celebrated in Assam.

  2. Ganesh Chaturthi is celebrated on the largest scale in which state?

    Show answer

    Maharashtra. The ten-day festival is famous in Maharashtra.

  3. Kathakali is a classical dance of which state?

    Show answer

    Kerala. Kathakali comes from Kerala.

  4. Bharatanatyam is a classical dance from which state?

    Show answer

    Tamil Nadu. Bharatanatyam comes from Tamil Nadu.

  5. Followers of which faith celebrate Guru Nanak Jayanti?

    Show answer

    Sikhism. It marks the birth of Guru Nanak, the first Sikh Guru.

  6. In which city is the Golden Temple?

    Show answer

    Amritsar. Sri Harmandir Sahib, the Golden Temple, is in Amritsar.

  7. Lohri, celebrated with a bonfire, falls in which month?

    Show answer

    January. Lohri is celebrated on 13 January.

  8. Navratri is celebrated over how many nights?

    Show answer

    Nine. Navratri means nine nights.

  9. Garba is a folk dance of which state?

    Show answer

    Gujarat. Garba is danced in Gujarat, especially during Navratri.

  10. The Hornbill Festival is held in which state?

    Show answer

    Nagaland. The Hornbill Festival is held in Nagaland every December.
