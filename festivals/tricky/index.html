Festivals and Culture quiz: tricky round

Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Kathak is a classical dance that grew in which part of India?

    Show answer

    North India. Kathak developed in north India, with famous schools in Lucknow, Jaipur and Banaras.

  2. Odissi is a classical dance of which state?

    Show answer

    Odisha. Odissi comes from Odisha.

  3. The famous camel fair of Pushkar is held in which state?

    Show answer

    Rajasthan. The Pushkar fair is held in Rajasthan.

  4. At any one place, the Kumbh Mela is held once in how many years?

    Show answer

    12. The full Kumbh returns to each place every 12 years.

  5. Losar, a new year festival, is celebrated mainly by which community?

    Show answer

    Tibetan Buddhists. Losar is the Tibetan new year, celebrated in Ladakh, Sikkim and other Himalayan areas.

  6. Chhath Puja is offered mainly to which deity?

    Show answer

    The Sun god. Chhath is the worship of Surya, the Sun, and Chhathi Maiya.

  7. Madhubani painting comes from which state?

    Show answer

    Bihar. Madhubani, or Mithila, painting comes from Bihar.

  8. Warli painting is a tribal art of which state?

    Show answer

    Maharashtra. Warli art comes from Maharashtra.

  9. Pandit Ravi Shankar was a master of which instrument?

    Show answer

    Sitar. Ravi Shankar took the sitar to the whole world.

  10. Thrissur Pooram, known for its decorated elephants, is a festival of which state?

    Show answer

    Kerala. Thrissur Pooram is held in Kerala.
