Food quiz: easy round

Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Idli batter is made mainly from which two ingredients?

    Show answer

    Rice and urad dal. Idli is made from fermented rice and urad dal batter.

  2. Oranges and lemons are rich in which vitamin?

    Show answer

    Vitamin C. Citrus fruits are rich in vitamin C.

  3. Paneer is made from what?

    Show answer

    Milk. Paneer is made by curdling milk.

  4. What is the main ingredient of a roti or chapati?

    Show answer

    Wheat flour. Roti is made from whole wheat flour, called atta.

  5. Which spice gives many Indian dishes a yellow colour?

    Show answer

    Turmeric. Turmeric, or haldi, gives the yellow colour.

  6. Pizza first came from which country?

    Show answer

    Italy. Pizza comes from Italy, especially the city of Naples.

  7. Sushi is a traditional dish of which country?

    Show answer

    Japan. Sushi comes from Japan.

  8. Which fruit carries its seeds on the outside?

    Show answer

    Strawberry. The tiny seeds of a strawberry are on its skin.

  9. Jaggery, or gur, is most commonly made from the juice of which plant?

    Show answer

    Sugarcane. Most jaggery is made by boiling sugarcane juice. Some is made from palm sap.

  10. Rasgulla is made mainly from what?

    Show answer

    Chhena. Rasgulla is made from chhena, a fresh soft cheese, cooked in sugar syrup.
