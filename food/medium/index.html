Food quiz: medium round

Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Which is the costliest spice by weight?

    Show answer

    Saffron. Saffron, or kesar, is the costliest spice.

  2. Rajma is the Hindi name for which bean?

    Show answer

    Kidney bean. Rajma is the red kidney bean.

  3. Dhokla is a well-known snack of which state?

    Show answer

    Gujarat. Dhokla comes from Gujarat.

  4. Litti chokha is a traditional dish of which state?

    Show answer

    Bihar. Litti chokha is from Bihar and nearby areas.

  5. What is the main ingredient of guacamole?

    Show answer

    Avocado. Guacamole is a Mexican dip made from mashed avocado.

  6. Marzipan is made from sugar and which nut?

    Show answer

    Almond. Marzipan is a paste of ground almonds and sugar.

  7. Besan is flour made from what?

    Show answer

    Gram (chana). Besan is made by grinding chana dal.

  8. Makki di roti with sarson da saag is a winter favourite of which state?

    Show answer

    Punjab. It is a classic Punjabi winter meal.

  9. Chocolate is made from the beans of which tree?

    Show answer

    Cacao. Chocolate is made from cacao beans.

  10. Tofu is made from which crop?

    Show answer

    Soybean. Tofu is made from soy milk.
