Food quiz: tricky round

Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. What is hing called in English?

    Show answer

    Asafoetida. Hing is asafoetida, a strong-smelling gum used in tiny amounts.

  2. Carrots are a good source of which vitamin?

    Show answer

    Vitamin A. Carrots have beta-carotene, which the body turns into vitamin A.

  3. Ragi is the Indian name for which grain?

    Show answer

    Finger millet. Ragi is finger millet. Bajra is pearl millet and jowar is sorghum.

  4. Sabudana is made from the starch of which plant?

    Show answer

    Tapioca (cassava). Sabudana pearls are made from tapioca root starch.

  5. Kahwa, a tea with saffron and spices, comes from which region?

    Show answer

    Kashmir. Kahwa is a traditional drink of Kashmir.

  6. Vindaloo is a dish linked to which Indian state?

    Show answer

    Goa. Vindaloo comes from Goa and has Portuguese roots.

  7. Which substance makes chillies taste hot?

    Show answer

    Capsaicin. Capsaicin gives chillies their heat. Curcumin is in turmeric.

  8. Mishti doi is a sweet curd from which state?

    Show answer

    West Bengal. Mishti doi is a Bengali sweet.

  9. Which dal is most commonly used in sambar?

    Show answer

    Toor dal. Sambar is usually made with toor dal, also called arhar.

  10. Poha is made from what?

    Show answer

    Flattened rice. Poha is rice that has been parboiled and flattened.
