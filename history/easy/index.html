History quiz: easy round

Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Who was the first Prime Minister of India?

    Show answer

    Jawaharlal Nehru. Jawaharlal Nehru became Prime Minister in 1947.

  2. In which year did India become independent?

    Show answer

    1947. India became independent on 15 August 1947.

  3. Who is called the Father of the Nation in India?

    Show answer

    Mahatma Gandhi. Mahatma Gandhi is called the Father of the Nation.

  4. Which Mughal emperor built the Taj Mahal?

    Show answer

    Shah Jahan. Shah Jahan built it in memory of Mumtaz Mahal.

  5. Who was the first President of India?

    Show answer

    Rajendra Prasad. Dr Rajendra Prasad was President from 1950 to 1962.

  6. Gandhiji's march to Dandi in 1930 was a protest against the tax on what?

    Show answer

    Salt. The Salt March was a protest against the salt law.

  7. Who is known as the Iron Man of India?

    Show answer

    Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. Sardar Patel united the princely states with India.

  8. Who led the Azad Hind Fauj, the Indian National Army?

    Show answer

    Subhas Chandra Bose. Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose led the Indian National Army.

  9. Which queen of Jhansi fought in the revolt of 1857?

    Show answer

    Rani Lakshmibai. Rani Lakshmibai of Jhansi fought in 1857 and 1858.

  10. Emperor Ashoka belonged to which dynasty?

    Show answer

    Maurya. Ashoka was the third Mauryan emperor.
