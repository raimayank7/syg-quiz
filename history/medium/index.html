History quiz: medium round

Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. In which year was the Battle of Plassey fought?

    Show answer

    1757. The Battle of Plassey was fought in 1757.

  2. In which year did the Quit India Movement begin?

    Show answer

    1942. The Quit India Movement began in August 1942.

  3. Who founded the Mughal Empire in India?

    Show answer

    Babur. Babur founded the empire after the First Battle of Panipat.

  4. Who was the chairman of the Drafting Committee of the Constitution?

    Show answer

    B. R. Ambedkar. Dr B. R. Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee.

  5. The Jallianwala Bagh tragedy of 1919 took place in which city?

    Show answer

    Amritsar. It took place in Amritsar on 13 April 1919.

  6. The revolt often called the First War of Independence began in which year?

    Show answer

    1857. The revolt began in 1857.

  7. Who was the first woman Prime Minister of India?

    Show answer

    Indira Gandhi. Indira Gandhi became Prime Minister in 1966.

  8. In 1911 it was announced that the capital of British India would move from Calcutta to which city?

    Show answer

    Delhi. The move to Delhi was announced at the Delhi Durbar of 1911.

  9. Harappa and Mohenjo-daro were cities of which civilisation?

    Show answer

    Indus Valley. They were cities of the Indus Valley, or Harappan, civilisation.

  10. The Indian National Congress was founded in which year?

    Show answer

    1885. The Congress was founded in 1885.
