History quiz: tricky round

Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. In which year did the Second World War end?

    Show answer

    1945. The war ended in 1945.

  2. In which year did the French Revolution begin?

    Show answer

    1789. The French Revolution began in 1789.

  3. Who introduced printing with movable metal type in Europe?

    Show answer

    Johannes Gutenberg. Gutenberg set up his press around 1450.

  4. In which year was the First Battle of Panipat fought?

    Show answer

    1526. Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi at Panipat in 1526.

  5. Who was the only Indian to be Governor-General of India?

    Show answer

    C. Rajagopalachari. C. Rajagopalachari held the post from 1948 to 1950.

  6. In which year did Vasco da Gama reach Calicut by sea?

    Show answer

    1498. Vasco da Gama reached Calicut in 1498.

  7. Which Chola king built the Brihadeeswara Temple at Thanjavur?

    Show answer

    Rajaraja Chola I. Rajaraja Chola I built the great temple about a thousand years ago.

  8. Which Mughal emperor built Fatehpur Sikri?

    Show answer

    Akbar. Akbar built Fatehpur Sikri near Agra.

  9. In which year did the Berlin Wall fall?

    Show answer

    1989. The Berlin Wall fell in November 1989.

  10. Who founded the Khalsa in 1699?

    Show answer

    Guru Gobind Singh. Guru Gobind Singh founded the Khalsa at Anandpur Sahib.
