Three ways to use a round

  1. Play alone. Tap an option on any round page. You see the right answer, a short note and your score.
  2. Play in a WhatsApp group. Tap "Send on WhatsApp" to post the ten questions with options A to D. When the time is up, paste the answer key.
  3. Play in a room. Read the questions aloud in class, at a party or at an office break, and keep points on the scoreboard. You can also print a round with its answers.

What kind of questions are these?

Each topic has an easy round, a medium round and a tricky round, so mixed-age groups can play together. Every question has four options and one clear, checked answer, with a one-line note that says why. Questions about records that keep changing are left out, so a round you save today is still right next year.

Questions people ask

Are the quizzes free?

Yes. Every round is free to play, copy, print and send. There is no login.

How do I run a quiz in a WhatsApp group?

Open a round, tap "Send on WhatsApp" and choose your group. Ask everyone to reply with answers like 1A 2C 3B before a set time. Then tap "Copy answer key" and paste it in the group. The page "Host a quiz" explains scoring and tie-breakers.

Are the answers checked?

Every question has one clear answer that you can find in a school textbook or an encyclopaedia. Records that change often, such as sports records, are left out on purpose. If you find a mistake, tell us through the Contact page and we will fix it.

Can I use these questions in my school or office?

Yes. Teachers, quiz clubs and offices may use the rounds freely for their own events. Please do not republish the whole collection as your own.

How many questions are there?

360 questions in 12 topics. Each topic has three rounds of ten questions.
