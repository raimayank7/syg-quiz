India quiz: easy round

Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. What is the national animal of India?

    Show answer

    Tiger. The tiger is the national animal. The lions are on the national emblem.

  2. Which bird is the national bird of India?

    Show answer

    Peacock. The Indian peafowl, called the peacock, is the national bird.

  3. How many spokes does the Ashoka Chakra on the national flag have?

    Show answer

    24. The navy blue wheel in the middle of the flag has 24 spokes.

  4. What is the capital of India?

    Show answer

    New Delhi. New Delhi is the capital.

  5. Which city is called the Pink City?

    Show answer

    Jaipur. Jaipur in Rajasthan is known as the Pink City for the colour of its old buildings.

  6. On which date does India celebrate Republic Day?

    Show answer

    26 January. The Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950.

  7. Which is the largest state of India by area?

    Show answer

    Rajasthan. Rajasthan is the largest state by area.

  8. What is the national fruit of India?

    Show answer

    Mango. The mango is the national fruit.

  9. Who wrote the national anthem, Jana Gana Mana?

    Show answer

    Rabindranath Tagore. Rabindranath Tagore wrote Jana Gana Mana.

  10. Which river is the longest river in India?

    Show answer

    Ganga. The Ganga is the longest river that flows in India.
