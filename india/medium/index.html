India quiz: medium round

Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Which is the smallest state of India by area?

    Show answer

    Goa. Goa is the smallest state by area.

  2. Which state has the longest coastline on the mainland?

    Show answer

    Gujarat. Gujarat has the longest mainland coastline.

  3. Who wrote the national song, Vande Mataram?

    Show answer

    Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay. It appears in his novel Anandamath.

  4. What is the highest civilian award of India?

    Show answer

    Bharat Ratna. The Bharat Ratna is the highest civilian award.

  5. In which year was the rupee symbol ₹ adopted?

    Show answer

    2010. The Government of India adopted the symbol in 2010.

  6. Which city is often called the Silicon Valley of India?

    Show answer

    Bengaluru. Bengaluru is known for its software and technology companies.

  7. What is the national aquatic animal of India?

    Show answer

    Ganges river dolphin. The Ganges river dolphin was declared the national aquatic animal.

  8. In which state is the Sun Temple of Konark?

    Show answer

    Odisha. The Konark Sun Temple is in Odisha.

  9. Which line of latitude passes through the middle of India?

    Show answer

    Tropic of Cancer. The Tropic of Cancer crosses eight Indian states.

  10. Who was the first Indian to travel to space?

    Show answer

    Rakesh Sharma. Rakesh Sharma flew to space in 1984.
