India quiz: tricky round

Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Which state was formed in 2014?

    Show answer

    Telangana. Telangana was formed on 2 June 2014. The other three were formed in 2000.

  2. Indian Standard Time is how far ahead of UTC?

    Show answer

    5 hours 30 minutes. IST is UTC plus 5 hours 30 minutes.

  3. The national emblem is adapted from the Lion Capital of Ashoka at which place?

    Show answer

    Sarnath. The Lion Capital stood at Sarnath, near Varanasi.

  4. The words Satyameva Jayate come from which text?

    Show answer

    Mundaka Upanishad. The motto, meaning truth alone triumphs, is from the Mundaka Upanishad.

  5. Which is the oldest mountain range in India?

    Show answer

    Aravalli. The Aravalli range is among the oldest fold mountains in the world.

  6. Which Indian state has the most districts?

    Show answer

    Uttar Pradesh. Uttar Pradesh has the most districts of any state.

  7. The Sundarbans mangrove forest in India lies in which state?

    Show answer

    West Bengal. The Indian part of the Sundarbans is in West Bengal.

  8. India's first passenger train ran in 1853 from Bombay to which place?

    Show answer

    Thane. The train ran from Bori Bunder in Bombay to Thane on 16 April 1853.

  9. Which town is at the southern tip of the Indian mainland?

    Show answer

    Kanyakumari. Kanyakumari is at the southern tip of the mainland.

  10. Which river is known as Dakshin Ganga?

    Show answer

    Godavari. The Godavari is called the Ganga of the south.
