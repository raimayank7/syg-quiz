Kids quiz: round 1

Ten very easy questions for the youngest players. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. What colour is the sky on a clear day?

    Show answer

    Blue. On a clear day the sky looks blue.

  2. How many days are there in a week?

    Show answer

    7. Monday to Sunday make seven days.

  3. How many months are there in a year?

    Show answer

    12. January to December make twelve months.

  4. Which animal says moo?

    Show answer

    Cow. A cow says moo.

  5. What comes after 9?

    Show answer

    10. After nine comes ten.

  6. How many legs does a cat have?

    Show answer

    4. A cat has four legs.

  7. Which colour do you get when you mix red and yellow?

    Show answer

    Orange. Red and yellow make orange.

  8. Which of these is a fruit?

    Show answer

    Apple. An apple is a fruit. The others are vegetables.

  9. In which direction does the Sun rise?

    Show answer

    East. The Sun rises in the east and sets in the west.

  10. What shape is a football?

    Show answer

    Round. A football is round, like a sphere.
