Kids quiz: round 2

Ten more easy questions about days, seasons and everyday things. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Which month can have 28 or 29 days?

    Show answer

    February. February has 28 days, and 29 in a leap year.

  2. How many hours are there in a day?

    Show answer

    24. One full day has 24 hours.

  3. What is the opposite of hot?

    Show answer

    Cold. The opposite of hot is cold.

  4. Who brings letters to our homes?

    Show answer

    Postman. The postman brings letters.

  5. How many sides does a triangle have?

    Show answer

    3. A triangle has three sides.

  6. In India, which season comes after summer?

    Show answer

    Rainy season. After summer the monsoon rains come.

  7. What is a baby cow called?

    Show answer

    Calf. A baby cow is a calf.

  8. How many days are there in a normal year?

    Show answer

    365. A normal year has 365 days. A leap year has 366.

  9. Santa Claus comes on which festival?

    Show answer

    Christmas. Children wait for Santa Claus at Christmas.

  10. How many wheels does a tricycle have?

    Show answer

    3. Tri means three.
