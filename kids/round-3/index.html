Kids quiz: round 3

Ten questions on letters, numbers and colours for children who can read a little. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. How many letters are there in the English alphabet?

    Show answer

    26. A to Z make 26 letters.

  2. How many vowels are there in the English alphabet?

    Show answer

    5. A, E, I, O and U.

  3. Which colour do you get when you mix red and blue?

    Show answer

    Purple. Red and blue make purple.

  4. Which colour is at the top of the Indian flag?

    Show answer

    Saffron. Saffron is on top, white in the middle and green at the bottom.

  5. Which of these is the fastest?

    Show answer

    Aeroplane. An aeroplane is the fastest of these.

  6. Which animal has a trunk?

    Show answer

    Elephant. An elephant has a long trunk.

  7. How much is ten tens?

    Show answer

    100. 10 × 10 is 100.

  8. What does water turn into when it freezes?

    Show answer

    Ice. Water freezes into ice.

  9. Which part of the body do we hear with?

    Show answer

    Ears. We hear with our ears.

  10. How many minutes are there in one hour?

    Show answer

    60. One hour has 60 minutes.
