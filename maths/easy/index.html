Maths and Logic quiz: easy round

Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. What is 7 × 8?

    Show answer

    56. Seven eights are fifty-six.

  2. How many sides does a hexagon have?

    Show answer

    6. A hexagon has six sides.

  3. What is half of 250?

    Show answer

    125. 250 divided by 2 is 125.

  4. What is 15 + 27?

    Show answer

    42. 15 plus 27 is 42.

  5. How many minutes are there in 2 hours?

    Show answer

    120. 60 minutes twice is 120.

  6. What comes next: 2, 4, 6, 8, ...?

    Show answer

    10. The even numbers go up by 2.

  7. How many zeros are there in one lakh?

    Show answer

    5. One lakh is 1,00,000.

  8. How many things make a dozen?

    Show answer

    12. A dozen is twelve.

  9. What is 100 − 37?

    Show answer

    63. 100 minus 37 is 63.

  10. What do the three angles of a triangle add up to?

    Show answer

    180 degrees. The angles of any triangle add up to 180 degrees.
