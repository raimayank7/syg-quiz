Quiz

Maths and Logic quiz questions with answers

Thirty questions with numbers: quick mental sums, patterns, and a tricky round of puzzles where the first answer that comes to mind is often wrong. No calculators.

  • Easy round

    Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more.

    • What is 7 × 8?
    • How many sides does a hexagon have?
    • What is half of 250?
    Play this round

  • Medium round

    Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams.

    • What is 12 × 12?
    • What is the square root of 169?
    • What is 25% of 200?
    Play this round

  • Tricky round

    Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish.

    • Five machines make 5 toys in 5 minutes. How long do 100 machines take to make 100 toys?
    • A bat and a ball cost ₹110 together. The bat costs ₹100 more than the ball. What does the ball cost?
    • What comes next: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ...?
    Play this round

How to play

Open a round and tap the option you think is right. The answer and a short note appear at once, and your score is counted at the bottom. To play with a group, send the round on WhatsApp and paste the answer key when the time is up.

Who this topic suits

Maths and Logic suits players of about nine and above who like to think: quick sums, number patterns and puzzles that sound harder than they are. No formula beyond school arithmetic is needed. It is a good topic for a class, for a family with children at different levels and for adults who enjoy being tricked by a simple question.

Hosting tips

Give every team paper and a pencil, and allow more time than for other topics: thirty to forty-five seconds for a question. Read each question twice, slowly, since a missed word changes a sum. Ask the team that answers to explain how it got there; hearing the method is how the other teams learn, and it shows up lucky guesses. Calculators and phones stay face down. The Tricky round is best played with everyone writing the answer silently and showing it together.
