Maths and Logic quiz: medium round

Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. What is 12 × 12?

    Show answer

    144. Twelve twelves are 144.

  2. What is the square root of 169?

    Show answer

    13. 13 × 13 is 169.

  3. What is 25% of 200?

    Show answer

    50. A quarter of 200 is 50.

  4. How many zeros are there in one crore?

    Show answer

    7. One crore is 1,00,00,000.

  5. What comes next: 1, 4, 9, 16, ...?

    Show answer

    25. These are the square numbers. 5 × 5 is 25.

  6. What is the value of pi to two decimal places?

    Show answer

    3.14. Pi is about 3.14159.

  7. What number is L in Roman numerals?

    Show answer

    50. L is 50, C is 100 and D is 500.

  8. Which of these is a prime number?

    Show answer

    29. 29 can be divided only by 1 and itself.

  9. A train runs at 60 km per hour for two and a half hours. How far does it go?

    Show answer

    150 km. 60 × 2.5 is 150.

  10. What is the perimeter of a square with a side of 9 cm?

    Show answer

    36 cm. Four sides of 9 cm make 36 cm. The area is 81 square cm.
