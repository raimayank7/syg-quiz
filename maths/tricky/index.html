Maths and Logic quiz: tricky round

Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Five machines make 5 toys in 5 minutes. How long do 100 machines take to make 100 toys?

    Show answer

    5 minutes. Each machine makes one toy in 5 minutes, so 100 machines make 100 toys in 5 minutes.

  2. A bat and a ball cost ₹110 together. The bat costs ₹100 more than the ball. What does the ball cost?

    Show answer

    ₹5. Ball ₹5 and bat ₹105. If the ball were ₹10, the total would be ₹120.

  3. What comes next: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ...?

    Show answer

    13. Each number is the sum of the two before it.

  4. How many times do the hands of a clock overlap in 12 hours?

    Show answer

    11. They overlap 11 times, because there is only one overlap between 11 and 1.

  5. What is 2 to the power of 10?

    Show answer

    1024. 2 multiplied by itself ten times is 1024.

  6. Which is the smallest prime number?

    Show answer

    2. 2 is the smallest prime, and the only even one. 1 is not a prime.

  7. A farm has hens and goats: 10 heads and 28 legs in all. How many goats are there?

    Show answer

    4. 4 goats and 6 hens give 16 + 12 = 28 legs.

  8. What is 15% of 80?

    Show answer

    12. 10% is 8 and 5% is 4, so 15% is 12.

  9. If today is Wednesday, which day will it be 100 days from now?

    Show answer

    Friday. 100 days is 14 weeks and 2 days. Two days after Wednesday is Friday.

  10. How many squares of all sizes are there on a chessboard?

    Show answer

    204. Counting 1×1 up to 8×8 squares gives 64 + 49 + 36 + 25 + 16 + 9 + 4 + 1 = 204.
