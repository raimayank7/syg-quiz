Quick quiz round

Choose your topics and the number of questions. The site picks random questions from all 360, so every round is new.

How the quick round works

  • Tap the topics you want. Green means chosen.
  • Tap "Start the round". Answer by tapping an option; the right answer and a note appear at once.
  • To play with a group, use "Send questions on WhatsApp" and later "Copy answer key".
  • Want fixed rounds instead? Go to all topics.
