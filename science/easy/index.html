Science quiz: easy round

Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. What is the chemical formula of water?

    Show answer

    H₂O. A water molecule has two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom.

  2. Which gas do plants take in to make their food?

    Show answer

    Carbon dioxide. Plants take in carbon dioxide and give out oxygen.

  3. At what temperature does water boil at sea level?

    Show answer

    100 °C. Pure water boils at 100 degrees Celsius at sea level.

  4. Which force pulls objects towards the Earth?

    Show answer

    Gravity. Gravity pulls everything towards the centre of the Earth.

  5. Which is the nearest star to the Earth?

    Show answer

    The Sun. The Sun is a star, and it is the nearest one.

  6. Ice is water in which state?

    Show answer

    Solid. Ice is solid water.

  7. Which gas do we need to breathe in to stay alive?

    Show answer

    Oxygen. Our bodies need oxygen from the air.

  8. Which instrument measures temperature?

    Show answer

    Thermometer. A thermometer measures temperature.

  9. How many colours are there in a rainbow?

    Show answer

    Seven. Violet, indigo, blue, green, yellow, orange and red.

  10. Which metal is strongly attracted by a magnet?

    Show answer

    Iron. Iron is magnetic. Gold, copper and aluminium are not.
