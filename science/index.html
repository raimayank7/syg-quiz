Quiz

Science quiz questions with answers

Thirty science questions from the kitchen, the classroom and the lab: water and air, metals and gases, units and famous discoveries. The easy round suits primary classes; the tricky round is for older students.

  • Easy round

    Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more.

    • What is the chemical formula of water?
    • Which gas do plants take in to make their food?
    • At what temperature does water boil at sea level?
    Play this round

  • Medium round

    Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams.

    • What is the chemical symbol for gold?
    • Which is the hardest natural substance?
    • About how fast does light travel in empty space?
    Play this round

  • Tricky round

    Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish.

    • Which metal is liquid at room temperature?
    • What is absolute zero on the Celsius scale?
    • Which is the lightest element?
    Play this round

How to play

Open a round and tap the option you think is right. The answer and a short note appear at once, and your score is counted at the bottom. To play with a group, send the round on WhatsApp and paste the answer key when the time is up.
