Science quiz: medium round

Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. What is the chemical symbol for gold?

    Show answer

    Au. Au comes from aurum, the Latin word for gold.

  2. Which is the hardest natural substance?

    Show answer

    Diamond. Diamond, a form of carbon, is the hardest natural substance.

  3. About how fast does light travel in empty space?

    Show answer

    3,00,000 km per second. Light travels about 3 lakh kilometres every second.

  4. What is the SI unit of electric current?

    Show answer

    Ampere. Current is measured in amperes.

  5. Who gave the theory of relativity?

    Show answer

    Albert Einstein. Albert Einstein published it in the early 1900s.

  6. What is the pH of pure water at room temperature?

    Show answer

    7. Pure water is neutral, with a pH of 7.

  7. Which gas makes up most of the air?

    Show answer

    Nitrogen. About 78 percent of air is nitrogen.

  8. What is the process by which green plants make food using sunlight?

    Show answer

    Photosynthesis. Plants make food from carbon dioxide and water using sunlight.

  9. What is the SI unit of force?

    Show answer

    Newton. Force is measured in newtons.

  10. Which element has the chemical symbol Na?

    Show answer

    Sodium. Na comes from natrium, the Latin name for sodium.
