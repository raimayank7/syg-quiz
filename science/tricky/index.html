Science quiz: tricky round

Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Which metal is liquid at room temperature?

    Show answer

    Mercury. Mercury is the only metal that is liquid at ordinary room temperature.

  2. What is absolute zero on the Celsius scale?

    Show answer

    −273.15 °C. Absolute zero, 0 kelvin, is −273.15 °C.

  3. Which is the lightest element?

    Show answer

    Hydrogen. Hydrogen is the lightest and most common element in the universe.

  4. Who discovered penicillin?

    Show answer

    Alexander Fleming. Alexander Fleming discovered penicillin in 1928.

  5. What does DNA stand for?

    Show answer

    Deoxyribonucleic acid. DNA carries the genetic instructions of living things.

  6. Sound cannot travel through which of these?

    Show answer

    A vacuum. Sound needs a material to travel through. A vacuum has none.

  7. C. V. Raman won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1930 for work on what?

    Show answer

    Scattering of light. The Raman effect is about how light changes when it is scattered.

  8. What is the chemical formula of common salt?

    Show answer

    NaCl. Common salt is sodium chloride.

  9. Which particle of an atom has a negative charge?

    Show answer

    Electron. Electrons are negative, protons are positive, neutrons have no charge.

  10. What is the unit of frequency?

    Show answer

    Hertz. Frequency is measured in hertz.
