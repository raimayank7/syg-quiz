Space quiz: easy round

Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Which planet is known as the Red Planet?

    Show answer

    Mars. Mars looks red because of iron-rich dust.

  2. Which is the largest planet in the solar system?

    Show answer

    Jupiter. Jupiter is the largest planet.

  3. What is the Earth's natural satellite?

    Show answer

    The Moon. The Moon goes around the Earth.

  4. Which planet is closest to the Sun?

    Show answer

    Mercury. Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun.

  5. How many planets are there in the solar system?

    Show answer

    8. There are eight planets. Pluto is now called a dwarf planet.

  6. Which planet is famous for its bright rings?

    Show answer

    Saturn. Saturn has the brightest and largest rings.

  7. Which planet is third from the Sun?

    Show answer

    Earth. The order is Mercury, Venus, Earth.

  8. What is the name of our galaxy?

    Show answer

    Milky Way. Our solar system is in the Milky Way galaxy.

  9. Who was the first person to walk on the Moon?

    Show answer

    Neil Armstrong. Neil Armstrong stepped on the Moon in July 1969.

  10. What do we call a scientist who studies stars and planets?

    Show answer

    Astronomer. An astronomer studies objects in space.
