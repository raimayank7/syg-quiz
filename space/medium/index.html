Space quiz: medium round

Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Which is the hottest planet in the solar system?

    Show answer

    Venus. Venus is hotter than Mercury because its thick air traps heat.

  2. What is the name of India's series of missions to the Moon?

    Show answer

    Chandrayaan. Chandrayaan means Moon craft.

  3. Who was the first human to travel to space?

    Show answer

    Yuri Gagarin. Yuri Gagarin went around the Earth in April 1961.

  4. About how long does sunlight take to reach the Earth?

    Show answer

    8 minutes. Sunlight takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds.

  5. In which year was Pluto reclassified as a dwarf planet?

    Show answer

    2006. The International Astronomical Union did this in 2006.

  6. What was the first artificial satellite sent into space?

    Show answer

    Sputnik 1. The Soviet Union launched Sputnik 1 in 1957.

  7. What was the name of India's first satellite, launched in 1975?

    Show answer

    Aryabhata. Aryabhata was named after the ancient astronomer.

  8. In which city is the headquarters of ISRO?

    Show answer

    Bengaluru. The Indian Space Research Organisation has its headquarters in Bengaluru.

  9. What mainly causes tides in the sea?

    Show answer

    The Moon's gravity. The pull of the Moon, with some help from the Sun, causes tides.

  10. Which planet spins almost on its side?

    Show answer

    Uranus. Uranus is tilted so much that it seems to roll around the Sun.
