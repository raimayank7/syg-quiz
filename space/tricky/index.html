Space quiz: tricky round

Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. On which date did Chandrayaan-3 land near the south pole of the Moon?

    Show answer

    23 August 2023. Chandrayaan-3 landed on 23 August 2023, now marked as National Space Day.

  2. In which year was Mangalyaan, the Mars Orbiter Mission, launched?

    Show answer

    2013. It was launched in November 2013 and reached Mars in September 2014.

  3. Who was the first woman to travel to space?

    Show answer

    Valentina Tereshkova. Valentina Tereshkova flew in 1963.

  4. Who was the first woman of Indian origin to go to space?

    Show answer

    Kalpana Chawla. Kalpana Chawla first flew on the space shuttle in 1997.

  5. Which is the largest moon in the solar system?

    Show answer

    Ganymede. Ganymede, a moon of Jupiter, is the largest.

  6. Olympus Mons, the tallest known volcano in the solar system, is on which planet?

    Show answer

    Mars. Olympus Mons is on Mars.

  7. A light year is a unit of what?

    Show answer

    Distance. A light year is the distance light travels in one year.

  8. Which star is nearest to the Sun?

    Show answer

    Proxima Centauri. Proxima Centauri is a little over four light years away.

  9. On which planet is one day longer than one year?

    Show answer

    Venus. Venus takes longer to spin once than to go around the Sun.

  10. About how often can Halley's Comet be seen from the Earth?

    Show answer

    Every 76 years. Halley's Comet returns about every 76 years. It was last seen in 1986.
