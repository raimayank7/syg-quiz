Sports quiz: easy round

Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. How many players does a cricket team have on the field?

    Show answer

    11. A cricket team has eleven players.

  2. Which sport is played with a shuttlecock?

    Show answer

    Badminton. Badminton is played with a shuttlecock.

  3. How many rings are there on the Olympic flag?

    Show answer

    Five. The flag has five linked rings.

  4. Sachin Tendulkar is famous for which sport?

    Show answer

    Cricket. Sachin Tendulkar is one of the great batters of cricket.

  5. How many players from one basketball team are on the court at a time?

    Show answer

    5. Five players from each team are on the court.

  6. Wimbledon is a famous tournament of which sport?

    Show answer

    Tennis. Wimbledon is a tennis tournament played on grass in London.

  7. How many squares does a chessboard have?

    Show answer

    64. A chessboard has 8 rows of 8 squares.

  8. How long is a standard football match, without extra time?

    Show answer

    90 minutes. Two halves of 45 minutes each.

  9. In kabaddi, how many players from each team are on the court?

    Show answer

    7. Seven players from each side are on the court.

  10. Major Dhyan Chand was a legend of which sport?

    Show answer

    Hockey. Dhyan Chand won three Olympic gold medals in hockey.
