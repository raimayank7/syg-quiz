Sports quiz: medium round

Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. In which year did India first win the men's Cricket World Cup?

    Show answer

    1983. India won at Lord's in 1983.

  2. Who captained India in that first World Cup win?

    Show answer

    Kapil Dev. Kapil Dev was the captain in 1983.

  3. The Summer Olympic Games are normally held every how many years?

    Show answer

    4. The Games are held every four years.

  4. About how long is a marathon race?

    Show answer

    42.195 km. A full marathon is 42.195 kilometres.

  5. Who won India's first individual Olympic gold medal?

    Show answer

    Abhinav Bindra. Abhinav Bindra won gold in shooting at Beijing in 2008.

  6. Which athlete was known as the Flying Sikh?

    Show answer

    Milkha Singh. Milkha Singh was a great 400 metres runner.

  7. Which country hosted the first modern Olympic Games in 1896?

    Show answer

    Greece. The 1896 Games were held in Athens.

  8. How many players from one volleyball team are on the court?

    Show answer

    6. Six players from each team are on the court.

  9. P. V. Sindhu has won Olympic medals in which sport?

    Show answer

    Badminton. P. V. Sindhu is a badminton player.

  10. How many Grand Slam tennis tournaments are played each year?

    Show answer

    4. The Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon and US Open.
