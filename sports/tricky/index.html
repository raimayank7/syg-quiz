Sports quiz: tricky round

Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. How long is a cricket pitch between the two wickets?

    Show answer

    22 yards. A pitch is 22 yards, about 20.12 metres.

  2. Who scored the first double century in men's one-day international cricket?

    Show answer

    Sachin Tendulkar. Sachin Tendulkar made 200 not out against South Africa in 2010.

  3. Viswanathan Anand became a world champion in which game?

    Show answer

    Chess. Viswanathan Anand has been world chess champion five times.

  4. Neeraj Chopra won Olympic gold at the Tokyo Games in which event?

    Show answer

    Javelin throw. He won the men's javelin gold, held in 2021.

  5. Which country won the first FIFA World Cup in 1930?

    Show answer

    Uruguay. Uruguay hosted and won the first World Cup.

  6. In table tennis, a game is played up to how many points?

    Show answer

    11. A game is won at 11 points, with a lead of two.

  7. M. C. Mary Kom is a champion of which sport?

    Show answer

    Boxing. Mary Kom has won six world boxing titles.

  8. Which Indian city hosted the Asian Games in 1951 and 1982?

    Show answer

    New Delhi. New Delhi hosted the first Asian Games in 1951 and again in 1982.

  9. In golf, what is a score of one under par on a hole called?

    Show answer

    Birdie. One under par is a birdie. Two under is an eagle.

  10. The Dronacharya Award is given to people in which role?

    Show answer

    Coaches. The Dronacharya Award honours outstanding sports coaches.
