World Geography quiz: easy round

Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Which is the largest continent?

    Show answer

    Asia. Asia is the largest continent by area and by population.

  2. Which is the largest ocean?

    Show answer

    Pacific Ocean. The Pacific is the largest and deepest ocean.

  3. Which is the largest country in the world by area?

    Show answer

    Russia. Russia is the largest country by area.

  4. Which is the highest mountain above sea level?

    Show answer

    Mount Everest. Mount Everest is the highest mountain above sea level.

  5. Which is the largest hot desert in the world?

    Show answer

    Sahara. The Sahara in Africa is the largest hot desert.

  6. What is the capital of Japan?

    Show answer

    Tokyo. Tokyo is the capital of Japan.

  7. Which country is shaped like a boot on the map?

    Show answer

    Italy. Italy looks like a boot kicking a ball, the island of Sicily.

  8. Which is the smallest country in the world?

    Show answer

    Vatican City. Vatican City, inside Rome, is the smallest country.

  9. The Great Barrier Reef is off the coast of which country?

    Show answer

    Australia. The reef lies off Queensland, Australia.

  10. What is the capital of France?

    Show answer

    Paris. Paris is the capital of France.
