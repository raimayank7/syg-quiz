World Geography quiz: medium round

Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. What is the capital of Australia?

    Show answer

    Canberra. Canberra is the capital. Sydney is the largest city.

  2. What is the capital of Canada?

    Show answer

    Ottawa. Ottawa is the capital of Canada.

  3. Which river flows through London?

    Show answer

    Thames. The River Thames flows through London.

  4. Mount Kilimanjaro is in which country?

    Show answer

    Tanzania. Kilimanjaro, the highest mountain in Africa, is in Tanzania.

  5. Which is the largest island in the world?

    Show answer

    Greenland. Greenland is the largest island. Australia is counted as a continent.

  6. Which canal joins the Mediterranean Sea and the Red Sea?

    Show answer

    Suez Canal. The Suez Canal in Egypt joins the two seas.

  7. Which country is called the Land of the Rising Sun?

    Show answer

    Japan. Japan is known as the Land of the Rising Sun.

  8. Which is the longest mountain range on land?

    Show answer

    Andes. The Andes run along the west of South America.

  9. What is the capital of Brazil?

    Show answer

    Brasília. Brasília has been the capital since 1960.

  10. Where is the deepest known point in the oceans?

    Show answer

    Mariana Trench. The Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench, in the Pacific, is the deepest known point.
