World Geography quiz: tricky round

Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish. Tap an option to answer, or send the whole round to your group.

  1. Counting its overseas territories, which country uses the most time zones?

    Show answer

    France. With its overseas territories, France uses 12 time zones.

  2. Which country was once known as Abyssinia?

    Show answer

    Ethiopia. Abyssinia is an old name for Ethiopia.

  3. What is the capital of New Zealand?

    Show answer

    Wellington. Wellington is the capital. Auckland is the largest city.

  4. Which continent has no countries?

    Show answer

    Antarctica. Antarctica has research stations but no countries.

  5. Which strait separates Asia from North America?

    Show answer

    Bering Strait. The Bering Strait lies between Russia and Alaska.

  6. Which country has the longest coastline?

    Show answer

    Canada. Canada has the longest coastline of any country.

  7. Lake Baikal, the deepest lake in the world, is in which country?

    Show answer

    Russia. Lake Baikal is in Siberia, Russia.

  8. Which is the driest desert outside the polar regions?

    Show answer

    Atacama. The Atacama Desert in Chile is the driest non-polar desert.

  9. Which two countries share the longest land border?

    Show answer

    Canada and the United States. The border between Canada and the United States is the longest.

  10. What is the capital of Türkiye (Turkey)?

    Show answer

    Ankara. Ankara is the capital. Istanbul is the largest city.
